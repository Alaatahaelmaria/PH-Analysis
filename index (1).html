08 — Key Findings

Conclusions & recommendations

01
PEF–FEV1 coupling
Near-perfect correlation (r = 0.941) confirms strong internal consistency between both respiratory measures across all participants.
02
Pain & lung function
FEV1–Pain (r = −0.834) and PEF–Pain (r = −0.764) are the strongest clinical findings — lower lung capacity is clearly linked to higher pain sensitivity.
03
BMI not a predictor
Despite 64% of participants having normal BMI, BMI showed negligible correlation with respiratory function (PEF r = 0.05; FEV1 r = 0.11).
04
Monotonic pain trend
Mean PEF fell from 229 L/min (pain score 1) to 61 L/min (score 6), a 73% decline. FEV1 followed an identical pattern.
05
Expand the study
With n = 14, results are suggestive, not conclusive. Larger samples with matched controls and longitudinal follow-up are recommended.
